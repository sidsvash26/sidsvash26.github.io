German Verb Forms Trainer

Practice conjugating common German verbs in different tenses.

This interactive game helps reinforce your memory of key verb forms required for basic German communication. It focuses primarily on present, perfect, and simple past tenses.

Challenge yourself and see how quickly you can conjugate verbs like ‘haben’, ‘sein’, ‘gehen’, and ‘sprechen’!

German Vocabulary Flashcards

Master German vocabulary with this interactive flashcard game.

Practice German A1 words, complete with example sentences. Choose between English-to-German or German-to-English translation, and focus your learning by selecting specific chapters.

Test yourself by marking cards as correct or wrong, track your score, and navigate easily through the deck. A great tool for reinforcing vocabulary for any learning level!
