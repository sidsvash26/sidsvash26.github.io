Züri Drive

Practise for the Swiss practical test on real streets: Zürich, Adliswil and the motorways around them. Drive from the driver's seat, share the road with trams, traffic and pedestrians, and get marked like an examiner would.

Pick a place and drive wherever you like. The examiner still marks you, and you can open the map (M) to jump somewhere else or set a destination.

What the examiner watches

  • Rechtsvortritt. No sign, no markings: traffic from the right goes first. Slow right down for every unmarked junction.
  • Kein Vortritt and STOP. The inverted triangle with shark-teeth means give way; at STOP the wheels must stop completely.
  • Trams. They have priority almost everywhere. Never pull across their path.
  • Fussgängerstreifen. Stop for anyone on, or waiting at, a yellow zebra crossing.
  • Kreisel. Give way to the left on entry, no signal going in, indicate right before your exit.
  • Speed. 50 in town unless signed, zones of 30 and 20, 120 on the motorway.
  • Signal and look. Indicate every turn and lane change; in the test route, shoulder checks count too.
  • Lights. Red and red-with-yellow both mean stop. Yellow means stop if you safely can.
  • Autobahn. Use the slip road to get up to speed, keep right, keep two seconds' distance.
